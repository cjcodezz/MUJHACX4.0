Meet Digital Humans

Teachers who teach the way you want them to

Think out loud in Hindi, Tamil, Bengali, Marathi, Telugu or English — code-mixed, the way you actually speak. Pick the teacher whose style suits you: the patient one, the blunt one, the one who talks like your group chat.

A tutor that watches how you learn

Not a chatbot with a face on it. Three things happen on every single turn.

Talk, don't type

Explain it the way you actually speak — Hinglish, code-mixed, half-finished. Nothing gets corrected into English first, because how you say it is half of what AI Tutor reads.

It can see when you are lost

Turn the camera on and your expression is read on your own device. Frustrated gets shorter. Confused gets one step back. No frame ever leaves your browser.

The teacher changes, not the volume

A wrong answer is a symptom. AI Tutor works out which rule in your head produced it — and if that teacher's method misses, a different one steps in and tells you why.
